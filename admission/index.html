1. Freeze Option

You accept the allotted college and branch with complete satisfaction and choose NOT to participate in subsequent rounds. Your seat is confirmed.

2. Float Option (Recommended)

You retain your currently allotted seat as insurance, but stay eligible for upgrade to ANY higher preference choice across other institutes in next rounds.

3. Slide Option

You accept the seat in the current college, but choose to upgrade only if a better branch opens up in the SAME college in subsequent rounds.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the AllSarkariJobs JoSAA College Predictor work?

The JoSAA College Predictor matches your All India Rank (AIR) or category rank against official Joint Seat Allocation Authority (JoSAA) and CSAB Round 6 opening and closing ranks from 2020 through 2026 across 23 IITs, 31 NITs, 26 IIITs, and Top GFTIs, accounting for Home State (50% quota), Other State quota, reservation category, and gender pool.

Does this college predictor require my phone number or registration?

No. The predictor operates 100% in your browser. We never ask for your phone number, email, or login credentials. Your data is never sold to private coaching or private colleges.

What is the difference between Freeze, Float, and Slide in JoSAA counselling?

Freeze means you accept the allotted seat and exit further rounds. Float means you accept the allotted seat but remain open to upgrade to ANY higher preference choice across other institutes in subsequent rounds. Slide means you accept the seat but upgrade only within the SAME institute.
